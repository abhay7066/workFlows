# Step 03 — Review & Optimize for Reach

## Input
- `01-strategy.md`
- `02-script.md`

## Task
Act as a strict short-form video growth strategist. Score the script, fix every weakness, and produce the **final** script.

### 1. Score the script
Rate each item from 1 to 5 and give a one-line reason:

| # | Criterion | What "5" looks like |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Hook strength | Stops the scroll in under 2s, makes a specific promise or creates curiosity |
| 2 | Audience fit | Uses the audience's words and pain points from the strategy |
| 3 | Single clear message | The viewer can repeat the core message after one watch |
| 4 | Retention / pacing | A visual change every 2–4s, no slow setup, no filler words |
| 5 | Show, don't tell | Value is shown visually, not just claimed |
| 6 | Works on mute | On-screen text tells the full story |
| 7 | Payoff | A satisfying result or "aha" before the CTA |
| 8 | CTA clarity | One action, matches the goal, easy to do |
| 9 | Rewatch / share value | Something worth saving, sending, or rewatching (a tip, a relatable moment, a loop) |
| 10 | Platform fit | Length, tone, and caption suit the target platform |

**Total: /50.**

### 2. Improve the script
- Fix every item scored 3 or below.
- If the total is below 40, revise and re-score. Do at most 2 revision rounds.
- Consider making the ending loop back to the opening (it encourages rewatches).
- Tighten the caption: put the first line first (it shows before "more"), the CTA, and the hashtags.
- Keep placeholders as placeholders. Do not invent facts to make the script "stronger".

### 3. Optional extras
- 2 alternative hooks for A/B testing.
- Suggested posting time and frequency for the platform, as a general guideline.
